Choosing what comes after graduation is a big decision. If you’re curious about how things work, like helping people and enjoy both technology and conversation, business analysis may be worth a closer look.

## Could business analysis suit you?

There’s no single type of business analyst. Many share a few habits, though. Do any of these sound like you?

- You ask “why?” and want to understand the answer.
- Friends ask you to help plan, organize or sort something out.
- You notice when something is confusing and think about how it could be clearer.
- You like working with people, and you’re interested in technology without wanting to code all day.

If you nodded along, explore further. The pages below show what the work involves.

## Learn how to learn

Post-secondary study asks more of you than high school: more independence, more projects and more to keep track of. We help you build a study strategy from the start.

The [ITBA Student Dashboard](/your-success/) is a Notion template for setting goals, organizing your notes and building a portfolio. That portfolio becomes a way to show employers how you think.
